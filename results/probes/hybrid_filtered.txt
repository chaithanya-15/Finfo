| Strategy | Alpha (Dense Weight) | Recall@5 | Recall@1 | MRR | Mean Max Overlap | Doc Hit Rate @ 5 |

| Pure Dense + Company Filter | 1.0 | 0.5088 | 0.2588 | 0.3578 | 0.5763 | 0.6053 |
| Hybrid + Company Filter | 0.7 | 0.4737 | 0.1784 | 0.2826 | 0.5461 | 0.5526 |
| Hybrid + Company Filter | 0.5 | 0.3874 | 0.1330 | 0.2273 | 0.4968 | 0.5965 |
| Hybrid + Company Filter | 0.3 | 0.2763 | 0.1038 | 0.1664 | 0.4239 | 0.5263 |

*Anchor verification & finding*: Pure dense + company filter achieves R@5 = 0.5088. BM25 hybrid fusion underperforms pure dense across all alpha values (R@5 drops monotonically from 0.5088 down to 0.2763 as BM25 weight increases), confirming that exact lexical keyword matching degrades financial table retrieval due to heavy terminology variance in SEC filings.
